    Simple budgeting habits

    • Follow the 50 / 30 / 20 split

      Half for needs, a third for wants, the rest saved.

    • Record the same day

      Entries added later are usually wrong or forgotten.

    • Review once a week

      A quick Sunday check keeps the month from slipping.

    • Watch the top category

      Cutting the biggest one moves the total the most.

    • Keep a small buffer

      Set the budget slightly below what you can afford.

    • Use the daily figure

      A per-day number is easier to follow than a monthly one.
